# 📊 Store Closure Proposal - Elevation Bikes

**Author**: Ali Abbas  
**Date**: May 19, 2025  
**Role**: Operations Manager (Team project for Elevation DA Course)

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project was conducted as the final group task in the **Elevation Data Analyst course**. The objective was to perform a deep drill-down into company sales data for **Elevation Bikes**, with a focus on uncovering inefficiencies and proposing actionable, data-driven solutions to improve overall profitability.

## 🎯 Business Goal

As Operations Managers, our mission was to:

- Ensure every region reaches an **average revenue of $350K**
- Identify underperforming stores and regions
- Minimize expenses and improve operational efficiency

## 🔍 Key Analyses

- Sales performance across different regions
- Store-level revenue trends over multiple quarters
- Staff impact on store sales

## 📉 Key Insight

📌 **3 stores consistently underperform** in sales over the past quarters, dragging down profitability and resource efficiency.

## ✅ Recommendation

We proposed:

- **Closing the 3 least profitable stores**
- **Reallocating staff and resources** to high-performing locations
- **Reducing operational costs** (rent, logistics, wages)
- Improving the company’s **overall profit margins**

## 📈 Expected Outcomes

- Better resource allocation
- Leaner operations
- Increased company-wide profitability
